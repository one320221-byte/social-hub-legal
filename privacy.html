Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Social Hub Kirill ("the app") is a personal desktop application used by a single individual to manage their own social media accounts and publish their own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile data (display name, avatar, follower, like and video counts, and statistics of the account's own videos). The app also uploads video files that the owner selects, using the TikTok Content Posting API.

How data is stored

Access tokens and keys are stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the developer. Data is sent only to the official TikTok APIs.

Sharing and selling

The app does not collect data about other people, does not share data with third parties and does not sell data.

Deleting data

The owner can remove a connected account inside the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, and can revoke access in TikTok settings.
